📋 Skill Gem Notes

Early boss kill order for a smooth experience:

Devourer(Mud barrow) > cold witch(Clearfell) > hut witch(Grelwood) > rust king(red vale)

Grab Rapid Attacks I after killing devourer and Elemental Armament I after hut witch.

Early Support gem order:

Rapid Attacks I > Elemental Armament I > Concentrated Area > 2nd Elemental Armament I

Early on when you lack support gems, you'll have to gem swap Elemental Armament I from lightning arrow to lightning rod on bosses, this is very important for boss damage. Or if you lack the strength requirement to use 2 at the same time.

Get Lightning Rod first and than Frost Bomb (should have both before devourer/cold witch, there's a chest in clearfell that always drops a lvl 1 skill gem).

Herald of Thunder is your still your ideal spirit gem because you can't swap to Ice Shot until lvl 31 and by than you'll have another spirit gem for Herald of Ice.

Pounce is used for mobility, grab it when you get a spare skill gem. I tend to drop it mid act 2 or act 3, as you run out of skill slots. You do need a talisman in your offhand for this to work, which is easy to find (use a low level one for low strength requirement). But this optional, can skip if you want less buttons.

---important!!!!

Because we're doing a mini skill swap at lvl 24, you'll need to save some uncut skill gems/supports for later. Do not use ANY lvl 6 or lvl 7 uncut gems before the swap.

📋 Equipment Notes

Early boss kill order for a smooth experience:

Devourer(Mud barrow) > cold witch(Clearfell) > hut witch(Grelwood) > rust king(red vale)

If you're more experience, feel free to skip devourer.

Early Support gem order:

Rapid Attacks I(LA) > Elemental Armament I(LA) > Concentrated Area(LR) > 2nd Elemental Armament I

In act 1 it's very important to have gear with flat damage to attacks (can be elemental or phys, just whatever gives you the highest dps). At lvl 11 you want to get a Warden Bow, you're just looking for the highest DPS one you drop or buy from the vendor. You're not looking to use any resources on this bow, as you want to make a 6 mod Recurve Bow at lvl 16 with 2 sockets. So save your artificers orb, exalts, regals and alchemy orbs.

Remember to get a Shortbow in Red Vale and reach lvl 5 before fighting rust king. Trans/aug both your bow/quiver whenever you acquire them.

Flask: Remember to constantly upgrade your life/mana flask throughout campaign, you can always buy a new one at the vendor when you meet the lvl requirements for the next tier (lvl 10, 16, 23, 30, 40, 50 and 60).

Quiver: One with +lvls and high amount of flat damage, the base isn't too important.

Also look at the vendor in town every 1-2 lvls for flat damage gloves, every source of flat damage for the build is massive in act 1 and act 2.

Boots: Movement speed on boots is also extremely good if you can find them, try to search the vendor every 1-2 lvls. I would never swap out movement speed boots, only replace them with other boots that have movement speed.

Other gear:

Ideally you should stick to evasion or evasion/es hybrid gear, but use whatever looks best at the time.

Helmet/belt/amulet are good slots to acquire attributes (str on belt, dex or int on helmet).

Besides that you're just looking for life/resist on gear, but flat damage is the most important on gloves/rings. High evasion chest is also quite good for defense if you find one.

!!!!Important!!!!!

Always salvage weapons with quality for whetstones and always salvage items with sockets for artificers orb, this is extremely important for gear upgrades throughout the campaign.

I also personally prefer to vendor all rares for gold to gamble in act 3 or act 4 for better gear.

🌳 Passive Tree

Take whatever attribute you need at the time, you'll default to dex if you don't need anything. You should also lookout for good attribute amulets or a strength belt to help out with requirements later on.

Ascendancy order:

Projectile Proximity Specialisation(point blank) -> Endless Munitions -> Gathering Winds

📋 Skill Gem Notes

Lesser Jeweller's orb priority - Snipe -> Ice Shot -> Freezing Mark -> Herald of Ice

Quality Priority - Freezing Mark -> Herald of Ice -> Snipe

This is the only big swap in campaign (at lvl 31 you'll swap Lightning Arrow for Ice Shot). While it is a bit of buttons at first, it's not that bad once you know what you're doing.

This swap requires at least 3 skill gems (one lvl 7 and two lvl 5 or 6 gems) and 4-5 support gems. So make sure you save these gems as you're lvling, you should usually have spare gems by lvl 24. Keep in mind the lvl 7 gem Has to be Freezing Mark and this skill is massive for freezing enemies, while giving a massive damage buff at the same time. If you have a 2nd lvl 7 gem, it should be Snipe.

Always keep Snipe at the highest available gem lvl you have access to and get Lightning Arrow to lvl 7 (stop upgrading after, because you swap to Ice Shot once you unlock a lvl 9 gem in act 3).

Frost Bomb Will only work if it's high enough lvl. At lvl 3 it'll affect enemies at lvl 25 or below, lvl 4 is 29 and lvl 5 is 32(So at lvl 5 it'll work for all of act 2). You'll drop this skill mid act 2 or start of act 3, as you'll lack the intelligence to keep lvling this skill, feel free to drop it when you swap to Snipe if you struggle with a lot of buttons.

If you feel like you're lacking freeze buildup, remember to constantly use Ice-Tipped Arrows as this activates Hail. You can also use Escape Shot to help with freeze buildup, but I never needed this in any of my runs.

Essentially the goal is to freeze the boss, press Barrage into a perfect window Snipe. (if you struggle with Snipe, you can takeout Window of Opportunity I for Elemental Focus, but it's not that bad).

Boss Rotation: use Ice-Tipped Arrows before the boss spawns, cast Freezing Mark, shoot Lightning Arrow 3-4 times, cast Freezing Salvo. If the boss is not frozen, use escape shot/salvo till it's frozen. Once the boss is frozen, cast Barrage into Snipe.

For general clear, I just use Ice-Tipped Arrows, Freezing Mark for the as extra buff and Lightning Arrow(because of Hail, lightning arrow will freeze normal monsters for the mark buff).

While I don't use it myself, Escape Shot can also help you build freeze if you feel lack you're struggling. But Freezing Salvo and Ice-Tipped Arrows should be more than enough. At lvl 31, freezing enemies become insanely easy because Ice Shot has massive freeze buildup, but Lightning Arrow with Ice-Tipped Arrows gets the job done.

Boots: Movement speed on boots is also extremely good if you can find them, try to search the vendor every 1-2 lvls. I would never swap out movement speed boots, only replace them with other boots that have movement speed.

Other gear:

Ideally you should stick to evasion or evasion/es hybrid gear, but use whatever looks best at the time.

Helmet/belt/amulet are good slots to acquire attributes (str on belt, dex or int on helmet).

Besides that you're just looking for life/resist on gear, but flat damage is the most important on gloves/rings. High evasion chest is also quite good for defense if you find one.

!!!!Important!!!!!

Always salvage weapons with quality for whetstones and always salvage items with sockets for artificers orb, this is extremely important for gear upgrades throughout the campaign.

I also personally prefer to vendor all rares for gold to gamble in act 3 or act 4 for better gear.

🌳 Passive Tree

Once you reach lvl 31, you'll want to setup your weapon swap points (you should have 10 after killing the monkey in act 3 in jungle ruins).

It's very simple, you want Freezing Mark on weapon swap 2 and take the mark nodes at the bottom. Every other skill should be weapon swap 1. (click on the skills detail in your skill menu to assigned weapon sets).

Projectile Proximity Specialisation(point blank) -> Endless Munitions -> Gathering Winds
